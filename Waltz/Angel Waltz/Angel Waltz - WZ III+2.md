# Angel Waltz
*Song by Vio Friedmann (Album: The Most Beautiful Songs - Magic, Track 8, 3:09)*

* **Choreographer**: Randy & Marie Preskitt
* **Rhythm**: Waltz
* **Phase**: III + 2 (Weave, Telemark)
* **Steplevel**:
* **Difficulty**: Average
* **Sequence**: Intro - AA* - BB* - C - Ending
* **Music**: Angel, Vio Friedmann

*Dance starts in left open facing position, man facing wall, lead feet free, waiting two measures*

# Intro (4 measures) [00:00]

> Wait 2; ; Together, Touch (to butterfly); Side, Touch;

> Waltz Away; Turn In (to left open); Back, Face, Side; Through, Side, Behind;

> Roll 3; Through, Chasse (to banjo); Maneuver; Impetus (to semi);

> Weave; (to banjo); Maneuver; Spin Turn;

> Box Finish; Turn Left & Right Chasse; Impetus (to semi); Through, Face, Close (to butterfly);
