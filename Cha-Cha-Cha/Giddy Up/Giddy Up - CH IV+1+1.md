# Giddy Up
*Song by Shania Twain (Album: Queen Of Me, Track 1, 2:42)*
 
* **Choreographer**: Rikki Lobato
* **Rhythm**: Cha-Cha-Cha
* **Phase**: IV + 1 + 1 (Sweetheart, Chase Full Turn with Triple Cha)
* **Steplevel**: 
* **Difficulty**: Average
* **Sequence**: Intro - A - Inter - BA - Inter - BCA - Bridge - B - Ending
* **Music**: Giddy Up, Shania Twain
 
*Dance starts in handshake position, man facing wall, lead feet free, waiting two measures*
 
# Intro (2 measures) [00:00]

> Wait 2; ; 

# A (8 measures)

> Flirt; ; Sweetheart; (twice, Lady in 4);

> Parallel Chase; ; Left Lariat; (Lady in 4 to face);

# Interlude (4 measures)

> Sand Step; (twice); Traveling Door; Cucaracha Right;

# B (6 measures)

> Chase Full Turn with Triple Chas; ; ; ;

> Half Basic; Underarm Turn (to handshake);

# A (8 measures)

> Flirt; ; Sweetheart; (twice, Lady in 4);

> Parallel Chase; ; Left Lariat; (Lady in 4 to face);

# Interlude (4 measures)

> Sand Step; (twice); Traveling Door; Cucaracha Right;

# B (6 measures)

> Chase Full Turn with Triple Chas; ; ; ;

> Half Basic; Underarm Turn (to left hand star);

# C (8 measures)

> Umbrella Turns; ; ; ;

> Crab Walks (to reverse); ; Aida; Switch Rock (with Giddy Up, to handshake);

# A (8 measures)

> Flirt; ; Sweetheart; (twice, Lady in 4);

> Parallel Chase; ; Left Lariat; (Lady in 4 to face);

# Bridge (1 measure)

> Two Side Closes;

# B (6 measures)

> Chase Full Turn with Triple Chas; ; ; ;

> Half Basic; Underarm Turn (to left hand star);

# Ending (8 measures)

> Umbrella Turns; ; ; ;

> Crab Walks (to reverse); ; Aida; Switch Rock (with Giddy Up);

*NOTE: Giddy Up action can be dancer choice such as making a hand motion like a lasso action over their head as they hear the “Giddy Up” at end of Part C and the Ending. This action should be done while the dancer is doing the side/close, side (Cha Cha) action of the Switch Rock.*
