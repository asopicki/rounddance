# Glimpse of us
*Song by Conor Maynard (Album: His Single, Track 1, 2:49)*

* **Choreographer**: Tom Hicks
* **Rhythm**: Hesitation Canter Waltz
* **Phase**: VI
* **Steplevel**:
* **Difficulty**: Average
* **Sequence**: Intro - A - Inter - BA - Inter - Ending
* **Music**: Glimpse of us, Conor Maynard

*Dance starts in open position, both facing wall, right foot free for both, waiting two measures*

# Intro (8 measures) [00:00]

> Wait 2; ; Sway Right & Left; Slow Side (lady develope);

> Slow Side; Quick Front Vine 3; Side & Cross; Man Side Close (twice) (lady pickup in 3);

# A (16 measures)

> Hover Telemark (to semi line); Through Serpiente; ; Slow Side Lock;

> Bounce Fallaway 4 (to banjo); Outside Change (to banjo); Cross Swivel (twice); Closed Wing;

> Telemark (to semi); Big Top (to reverse center); Contra Check & Switch (to line and wall); Step & Lady Ronde Lariat (to left open); Through Hover (to face);

> Change Sides (Lady Inside Turn); (to reverse) Through Swivel (twice, to butterfly); Slow Curl (to sidecar);

# Interlude (4 measures)

*Note: dance on 3 piano beats into the throwaway & dance on 3 piano beats out of the throwaway*


> Slow Back to Throwaway Oversway & Extend; ; Recover, Side (Lady curls, to sidecar); Box Finish;


*Note: The orignal Cuesheet says Recover, Through (Lady curls, to sidecar), but it is easier for the man to do a side step down line of dance to stay out of the way of the lady. This is also shown in the video demo of the dance https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fdaEicvAprA on Youtube*

# B (16 measures)

> Two Quick Viennese Turns; Drag Hesitation (with shape); Back Natural Turn; Checked Natural & Slip;

> Two Quick Viennese Turns; Drag Hesitation (with shape); Back Natural Turn; Checked Natural & Slip;

> Forward Canter Twist Vine 4; One Left Turn; Back Canter Twist Vine 4; Back Left Turn (to diagonal wall);

> Whisk; Left Whisk; Slow Unwind in 4 (to diagonal wall); ;

# A (16 measures)

> Hover Telemark (to semi line); Through Serpiente; ; Slow Side Lock;

> Bounce Fallaway 4 (to banjo); Outside Change (to banjo); Cross Swivel (twice); Closed Wing;

> Telemark (to semi); Big Top (to reverse center); Contra Check & Switch (to line and wall); Step & Lady Ronde Lariat (to left open); Through Hover (to face);


> Change Sides (Lady Inside Turn); (to reverse) Through Swivel (twice, to butterfly); Slow Curl (to sidecar);


# Interlude (4 measures)

*Note: dance on 3 piano beats into the throwaway & dance on 3 piano beats out of the throwaway*

> Slow Back to Throwaway Oversway & Extend; ; Recover, Side (Lady curls, to sidecar); Box Finish;

# Ending (15 measures)

> Two Quick Viennese Turns; Drag Hesitation (with shape); Back Natural Turn; Checked Natural & Slip;

> Two Quick Viennese Turns; Drag Hesitation (with shape); Back Natural Turn; Checked Natural & Slip;

> Forward Canter Twist Vine 4; One Left Turn; Back Canter Twist Vine 4; Back Left Turn (to diagonal wall);

> Whisk; Left Whisk; Extend;
