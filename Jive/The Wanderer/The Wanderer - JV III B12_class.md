# The Wanderer (YR Class) B12
*Song by Dion (Album: The Wanderer Greatest Hits, Track 1, 2:43)*

* **Choreographer**: Alexander Fartmann
* **Rhythm**: Jive
* **Phase**: III
* **Steplevel**: B12
* **Difficulty**: Average
* **Sequence**: Intro - ABC - AD - A - Ending
* **Music**: The Wanderer, Dion

*Dance starts in closed position, man facing wall, lead feet free, waiting two measures*

> Wait pick up notes & 2; ; Double Rock; Chasse Left & Right;
