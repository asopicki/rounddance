# Summer Place
*Song by Percy Faith (Album: The Essential Perciy Faith, Track 16, 2:21)*
 
* **Choreographer**: Bill & Martha Buck
* **Rhythm**: Slow Two Step
* **Phase**: III + 2 (Let Turn Inside Roll, Switches)
* **Steplevel**: 
* **Difficulty**: Average
* **Sequence**: Intro - AB - A(1-14) - Inter- A(1-12)B - Ending
* **Music**: Theme from 'A Summer Place', Percy Faith
 
*Dance starts in butterfly position, man facing wall, lead feet free, waiting two measures*

> Wait 2; ; Basic; ;

> Side Basic; Reverse Underarm Turn; Open Basic; (twice);
